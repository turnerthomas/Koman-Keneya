The Basics

What is a Master Plant?

In the Shipibo and wider Amazonian traditions, a master plant (planta maestra) is a plant regarded as a teacher and healer, not just a remedy. Each one is understood to carry its own spirit, character and gifts, such as strength, protection, an open heart, or the ability to bring clarity through dreams. Healers learn their craft by building a long, personal relationship with these plants.

What is a Dieta?

A dieta is the traditional way of getting to know a master plant. You take the plant in a prepared form, usually as a tea or juice, while living simply and apart from daily life, with a very plain diet and a set of restrictions. The goal is to become quiet and receptive so the plant can teach, heal and strengthen you. It is a commitment that goes well beyond the days spent with the plant. Through our commitment to the dieta, our sensitivity begins to increase and the world (nete) of the plants begins to open to us, allowing us to deepen our connection with them and begin receiving their icaros.
